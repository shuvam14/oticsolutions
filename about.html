About Otic Solutions

Getting to know the team and the mission behind better hearing in Kathmandu.

Manufacturer Authorized Oticon Hearing Aid Distributor in Nepal

Our Story

Otic Solutions Pvt. Ltd. was founded in Banasthali, Kathmandu, with a simple goal: make quality hearing care accessible to everyone in Nepal. As an authorized distributor for Oticon, we bring internationally trusted hearing technology to our community, paired with the kind of personal, patient attention that a hearing journey deserves. Since day one, we've focused on listening first — to your lifestyle, your concerns, and your hearing goals — before recommending a solution.

Who We Are

Our Mission

To improve the quality of life of people with hearing loss by providing genuine, high-performing hearing aids alongside honest consultation, careful fitting, and dependable after-sales support — every step of the way, for as long as our customers need us.
